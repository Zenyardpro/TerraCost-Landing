About TerraCost

Last updated: 13 September 2026

TerraCost is landscape estimating software. It turns a landscape design into a bill of quantities and a client-ready proposal — zone by zone, scope by scope — with GST or VAT handled per item and exports to PDF and Excel.

Not the Terraform tool. TerraCost has nothing to do with cloud infrastructure costs. It is built for landscape contractors, garden designers, landscape architects and quantity surveyors.

Built by people who do the work

TerraCost is made by Zenyard Studio, a Bengaluru-based green infrastructure and landscaping company (SOW Business Solutions Pvt Ltd). Over seven years and several hundred installations — homes, offices, hotels and layouts — we wrote our BOQs in spreadsheets, missed scopes, lost money on a project because a hidden scope never made it into the estimate, and rebuilt the same sheet for every new client. TerraCost is the tool we wished we had.

Two editions

  • TerraCost India — INR, GST per item, species library for Indian conditions.
  • TerraCost Gulf — USD pricing, VAT, GCC-native species, built for UAE and Saudi Arabia projects.

Part of TerraOS

TerraCost is the costing layer of TerraOS, a landscape intelligence platform that also includes TerraScope (site survey capture). Surveys captured in TerraScope can be imported directly into TerraCost, but TerraCost works fully standalone too.
